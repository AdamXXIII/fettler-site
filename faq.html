Delivery

How much is delivery?

£20 to anywhere in the UK, whatever the size of your order. I don't ship outside the UK.

Can I collect?

Yes, free, from the studio in Heaton, Newcastle upon Tyne. Choose collection at checkout and I'll message you to agree a time.

Lead time

How long will my order take?

Every shade is made to order. Allow up to 10 working days, then you get tracking details.

I need it by a certain date

Email me before you order and I'll tell you honestly whether I can do it.

Fixings and bulbs

Which fixing do I need?

Every shade fits a standard E27 fixing. It isn't included, so add one on the shade's page or use the one you have. Ceiling fixings come with 1m or more of adjustable cable. The plug-in wall fixing has a 4m cable with a switch.

My ceiling has an E14 or bayonet fitting

Add a ring adapter (£2) on the shade's page, or have your fixing changed to E27.

Which bulb do I need?

An LED bulb, up to 10W. That gives about the light of an old 100W bulb. Each shade's page lists the bulbs I sell that suit it. Don't use incandescent or vintage filament bulbs, and leave space between the bulb and the shade.

Can you tell me how to fit it?

No. Fitting must be done by a qualified electrician, and I can't give electrical or installation advice.

Light and shadows

Will it cast a shadow?

Yes. The shades are geometric, so they throw a patterned shadow across the room. If you'd like less of it, a frosted bulb softens it a lot.

Where shouldn't I hang one?

Not in bathrooms or damp rooms.

Returns

Can I return a shade?

Changed your mind? Tell Mike within 14 days of delivery, then send it back unused in its packaging within 14 days of that. You pay return postage unless it arrived faulty.

It arrived damaged

Email mike@fettler.co.uk straight away with a photo and I'll replace or refund it.
